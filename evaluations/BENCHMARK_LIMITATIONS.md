# Benchmark limitations

- The benchmark is public and vulnerable to repeated-development overfitting.
- It is project-authored rather than an externally validated examination.
- Most cases permit several acceptable answers and need semantic human review.
- The African-context family tests reasoning discipline, not representativeness of all countries, institutions, languages, or users.
- It is English-only and cannot support an African-language competence claim.
- It emphasizes research methodology and may not predict all official Math & Scientific Reasoning hidden prompts.
- It does not establish user demand, learning gains, safer research decisions, or impact.
- Human reviewers may disagree; attribution and adjudication must be retained.
- Results require triangulation with a local-only challenger set, general-capability retention tests, `llama.cpp` profiling, and official hidden prompts.
