# Model card — development draft

## Status

No final MethodBridge model exists. No final GGUF, quantization, public model URL, or model-selection approval has been recorded.

`Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B` with a proposed `Q5_K_M` test is a documentary hypothesis only. It is not a finalist, released artifact, or evidence-backed winner.

## Intended use

Offline research-methods and scientific-reasoning tutoring for postgraduate students and early-career researchers, with explicit uncertainty and accountable human review.

## Out of scope

Protocol, SAP, ethics, clinical, legal, regulatory, or institutional approval; patient-specific advice; fabricated citations; deceptive academic work; processing real confidential participant data in the public hackathon repository.

## Evidence boundary

- Simulation-proxy outputs are canned and unmeasured.
- The built-in keyword-overlap score is a non-authoritative automated proxy.
- Real model evidence requires an exact GGUF SHA-256 and pinned `llama.cpp` execution.
- Model selection requires qualified semantic adjudication.
- Official performance, efficiency, and thermal claims require the official ADTC profiler on a qualifying reference laptop.

## Known limitations

No evidence-backed candidate comparison, quantization comparison, official profiler measurement, African-language evaluation, hidden-prompt result, or user-outcome evidence is currently available.
