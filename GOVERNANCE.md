# Governance

MethodBridge Local is governed through the trace:

```text
work item → direct output → near-term outcome → intended impact
```

A model response is a direct output. It is not evidence that research quality, learning, or institutional capacity improved. Claims about outcomes require separate evaluation.

## Authority

Accountable humans retain authority over entrant eligibility, Theory-of-Change approval, final model choice, dataset admission, safety thresholds, public prompts, licensing, model hosting, and submission. The model may provide educational and methodological support but may not authorize scientific, ethical, clinical, legal, regulatory, or institutional decisions.

## Evidence

Material decisions require a source, confidence statement, alternatives, implementation consequence, verification test, and review trigger. Unknowns remain unknown. Conflicts remain visible.

## Protected decisions

See `governance/PROTECTED_DECISIONS.md`. Agents may propose changes but may not silently alter the primary user, ADTC domain, contribution boundary, held-out split, human-authority boundary, or submission-readiness rules.
