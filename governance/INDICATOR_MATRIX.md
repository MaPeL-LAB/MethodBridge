# Indicator matrix

## Direct technical indicators

MethodBridge quality score, critical error count, retention score, abstention quality, citation-fabrication rate, TPS, TTFT, peak RSS, steady RSS, temperature, crash rate, and repeatability.

## Outcome indicators for later study

Earlier identification of methodological problems, question quality, calibrated trust, changes after human review, false-confidence incidents, and user comprehension. These are not established by model benchmarks alone.
