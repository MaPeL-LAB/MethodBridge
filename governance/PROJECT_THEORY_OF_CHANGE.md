# Project Theory of Change

**Status:** populated draft; human approval not recorded in this recovered release.

## Problem hypothesis

Some postgraduate students and early-career researchers may lack timely, affordable, privacy-compatible research-methods support. The extent and form of this gap require user research.

## Intervention

A compact offline model provides structured explanations, critiques, clarification questions, uncertainty statements, and escalation guidance.

## Causal pathway

```text
licensed sources + compact model + qualified review
        ↓
curation, evaluation, conditional adaptation, quantization
        ↓
methodological explanations and critiques
        ↓
users may identify basic problems earlier and ask better questions
        ↓
with qualified human review, some decisions may become more coherent
        ↓
potential contribution to stronger research-methods capacity
```

## Contribution boundary

MethodBridge contributes educational and methodological decision support. It does not prove a study scientifically valid, cause improved research quality by itself, replace qualified supervision, or establish social or educational impact.
