The approval remains subject to the contribution boundary, licensed or explicitly

processing, production or institutional use, final model selection, tuning,
quantization, public-prompt selection, public hosting, rules acceptance, model
release, or Devpost submission.

## Decisions that still require accountable human approval

Accountable human approval remains required for:

- entrant identity and eligibility evidence;
- source and dataset admission and licensing;
- changes to the primary user, causal pathway, contribution boundary, or protected decisions;
- final base model and any adaptation strategy;
- final quantization and runtime configuration;
- final two public prompts and any African-language claim;
- public hosting, claims, release, and submission authorization;
- any processing of confidential, participant-level, or institution-controlled material;
- all scientific, ethical, clinical, legal, regulatory, and institutional decisions.

## Non-delegable authority

The model and development agents may propose, test, document, and surface
uncertainty. They may not approve research, ethics, clinical care, legal or
regulatory compliance, institutional decisions, or their own material changes.
No CI result, benchmark score, profiler output, or agent recommendation can
substitute for an attributable human decision.
